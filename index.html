What We Offer
Our Training Programs

Practical, results-driven courses led by certified industry experts — available live and online.

🏆

ISO 9001 Lead Auditor

Become a certified Lead Auditor for Quality Management Systems. Gain the skills to plan, conduct, and report third-party audits.

  • QMS principles & requirements
  • Audit planning & execution
  • Audit reporting & follow-up
  • IRCA-aligned curriculum
🔍

ISO 9001 Internal Auditor

Equip your team to conduct effective internal audits and drive continual improvement across your QMS.

  • Understanding ISO 9001:2015
  • Process-based approach
  • Internal audit techniques
  • Non-conformance management
🌱

ISO 14001 — Environmental

Understand and implement an Environmental Management System. Identify environmental aspects and reduce your organisation's impact.

  • ISO 14001:2015 requirements
  • Environmental aspects & impacts
  • Legal compliance obligations
  • EMS implementation roadmap
⛑️

ISO 45001 — H&S Management

Master the requirements of ISO 45001 and build a robust Occupational Health & Safety Management System for your workplace.

  • ISO 45001:2018 requirements
  • Hazard identification & risk assessment
  • Worker participation & consultation
  • OHS performance measurement
✈️

AS9100 — Aerospace QMS

Specialised training for the aerospace, defence, and space sectors. Understand AS9100 requirements and how they extend ISO 9001.

  • AS9100 Rev D requirements
  • Configuration management
  • Risk & opportunity management
  • First article inspection
📋

Combined ISO 14001 & 45001

An integrated programme covering both Environmental and OHS Management Systems — ideal for organisations implementing both standards.

  • Integrated management systems
  • Common requirements across standards
  • Combined audit strategies
  • Documentation & record control
